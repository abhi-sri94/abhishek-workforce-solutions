100% Labor Law & Regulatory Adherence

Statutory Compliance & Institutional Standards

ABHISHEK WORKFORCE SOLUTIONS adheres strictly to Indian statutory regulations and labor laws, protecting warehouse operators and manufacturing heads from vicarious liability and audit risks.

Minimum Wages Act, 1948

All worker wages are strictly disbursed in full alignment with the latest Central and State Government Minimum Wages notifications. Payments are transferred directly into verified worker bank accounts.

Gazetted Wage Adherence Direct Bank Transfers

E.S.I.C. & Provident Fund (P.F.) Coverage

Eligible workforce deployed at client facilities are covered under the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) for medical healthcare and the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) Act for social security.

ESIC Medical Benefits EPF Retirement Security

Contract Labour (R&A) Act, 1970

We maintain required statutory registrations and facilitate site-specific licenses and Form-V documentation under the Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, ensuring smooth operational clearances.

Authorized Registrations Form-V Regulatory Support

Audit-Ready Monthly Billing Submissions

Along with our monthly invoices, we submit complete compliance documentation including EPF & ESIC electronic challan returns (ECR receipts), wage registers, and signed attendance muster rolls.

ECR Payment Challans Zero Audit Risk for Clients
